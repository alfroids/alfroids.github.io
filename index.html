Welcome

About me

My main interest is understanding how different models balancing selection shape the diversity patterns inside and across populations. Using simulations, I explore how changing the rules of evolution impacts the outcome and compare the results to empirical population data to infer what might have happend in the real world.

I have a bachelor's degree in Molecular Sciences (2024) by University of São Paulo and am now pursuing my PhD in Genetics and Evolutionary Biology at University of São Paulo, under supervision of Prof. Diogo Meyer.

Outside the lab, I enjoy developing games and partaking in game jams. You'll find links to some of my projects below!

Research

What I study

How does diveristy emerge and is maintained? I am interested in different models of balancing selection and the patterns they generate across populations.

Symmetric heterozygote advantage at the human HLA loci

Which aspects of the HLA diversity patterns can be explained by a simple model of heterozygote advantage? And which cannot?

Epistasis and CEHs at the human MHC

How are some very long (~3Mb) haplotypes being maintained in populations at such high frequencies? Is epistasis involved?

Public goods altruism and the greenbeard genes of D. discoideum

Under which conditions can multiple tags of altruism be maintained in a population?
